# Fieldday India data-source roadmap

Build a **selective, cited discovery inventory** across all 28 states and 8 union territories. The starting atlas already represents all 36 jurisdictions; that is geographic representation, not district, theme, time-period or community completeness. An inventory record is not permission to visit. Keep the discovery atlas separate from the Adilabad and Bengaluru route catalogs.

## Source shortlist

These are sources to assess, not approved bulk imports. Prefer the institution responsible for the specific claim: a gazette for legal designation, an excavation report for a find and its context, a practitioner for a living tradition, and a site manager for current visitor access.

| Theme | Authoritative starting points and coverage | First use and cautions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Geology and geoheritage | [Geological Survey of India (GSI)](https://gsi.gov.in/) geoheritage and geological monuments; [Bhukosh map viewer](https://bhukosh.gsi.gov.in/Bhukosh/MapViewer.aspx); [National Geoscience Data Repository (NGDR)](https://geodataindia.gov.in/login). India-wide institutional scope, with layer, sheet, scale and survey-date variation. | Seed site names and designation from GSI; cite the relevant map sheet or report for interpretations. A regional geological contact is not a trail waypoint or a precise outcrop. Check layer terms and scale before copying polygons, maps, images or tiles. Suppress vulnerable fossil localities and unpublished outcrops. |
| Prehistoric archaeology | Archaeological Survey of India (ASI) [*Indian Archaeology: A Review*](https://asi.nic.in/pages/Publications/indianArchaelogyReports) and [publication series](https://asi.nic.in/pages/Publications/HQ); [Deccan College archaeology department](https://www.dcpune.ac.in/arch_historydept.html) and [Sharma Centre publications](https://www.sharmaheritage.in/research/publications) supplement ASI with project-level evidence. ASI volumes cover reported work in multiple regions and years, not every survey or site. | Start with dated volume/page citations and cross-check against final excavation reports and later scholarship. Keep stratigraphy, dating method, range and uncertainty separate from site discovery or report year. Surveys, excavations, and protection listings are different evidence. Use link-only references for scans and plates unless cleared; do not publish sensitive site coordinates, access paths or findspots. |
| History, monuments and museums | [ASI centrally protected sites](https://asi.nic.in/pages/Monuments) and [ASI museums](https://asi.nic.in/pages/Museums); [National Archives of India / Abhilekh Patal](https://nationalarchives.nic.in/en/online-records-national-archives-india/abhilekh-patal) and its [state archive directory](https://www.abhilekh-patal.in/Shared/Home/StateArchives); [UNESCO India World Heritage lists](https://whc.unesco.org/en/statesparties/in/). National holdings and designations are selective; add state archaeology registers, state gazetteers, district archives and museum catalogs for local coverage. | Cite notification or list edition for protection; keep inscribed and tentative UNESCO properties distinct. Cite archival series, file and page; availability of an index does not imply a scan exists or is reusable. Verify separate rights for archival copies, museum photographs, transcriptions and translations. |
| Biosphere and ecology | [Wildlife Institute of India (WII) EIACP](https://wii.gov.in/eiacp-program-center) for protected-area references; [Forest Survey of India, India State of Forest Report](https://fsi.nic.in/publications) for forest-cover context; [Botanical Survey of India Virtual Herbarium](https://ivh.bsi.gov.in/) and [Zoological Survey of India, Fauna of India](https://zsi.gov.in/fauna-of-india) for specimens and checklists; [GBIF India occurrence search](https://www.gbif.org/occurrence/search?country=IN) and the [India Biodiversity Portal](https://indiabiodiversity.org/) for additional occurrences. These differ in purpose, taxonomic reach, reporting dates and survey effort. | Verify protected-area status, boundaries and entry rules with the responsible department and notification. Distinguish biosphere reserve, protected area, forest-cover class and occurrence. An occurrence is not a promise of present-day wildlife or evidence of absence elsewhere. Use a versioned GBIF download DOI and publisher credits for approved reuse; review record and media terms separately. Generalize or withhold threatened-species locations, nests and roosts. |
| Anthropology and living heritage | [Anthropological Survey of India](https://ansi.gov.in/?lang=en), including *People of India* publications; [Census of India tables](https://censusindia.gov.in/census.website/data/census-tables) and [digital library](https://censusindia.gov.in/nada/index.php/home) for dated aggregate statistics and District Census Handbooks; [Ministry of Tribal Affairs](https://tribal.nic.in/) as an entry point to Tribal Research Institutes; [Sangeet Natak Akademi national inventory](https://sangeetnatak.gov.in/sections/ICH) and [IGNCA intangible heritage inventory](https://ignca.gov.in/divisionss/janapada-sampada/loka-parampara/intangible-cultural-heritage/inventory-on-the-intangible-cultural-heritage/). Surveys and nominations reflect their own dates, categories and selection; none is a current census of living practice. | Preserve publication terminology in citations but use contemporary, community-reviewed self-names in the atlas. Don't infer a living identity from geography or an old classification. Obtain consent for interviews, recordings and imagery; public nomination dossiers or consent letters are not blanket reuse permission. Keep private settlements, sacred places and restricted knowledge out of public maps and routes unless custodians explicitly approve release. |
| Culinary culture | IP India's [state-wise registered GI list](https://ipindia.gov.in/frontend/pdf/gi/registered/State_wise_Registered_GI_of_India.pdf) and [application records](https://search.ipindia.gov.in/GIRPublicSearch/); ICMR–National Institute of Nutrition [*Indian Food Composition Tables 2017*](https://www.nin.res.in/ebooks/IFCT2017.pdf); [Incredible India](https://www.incredibleindia.gov.in/en/) and state tourism features for leads. GI records cover registered products, not all foods; IFCT covers selected foods and nutrients, not cuisines or recipe origins. | Verify GI registration by application number and distinguish filing from registration. A GI region, a dish's cultural range and a meal venue are different records. Corroborate origin claims and credit regional variants and knowledge holders. Don't copy recipes, photos, GI logos or IFCT tables without checking rights; nutrient figures for an ingredient don't establish the nutrition of a dish. |
| Astronomy | IMD Positional Astronomy Centre (PAC) [*Indian Astronomical Ephemeris*](https://packolkata.imd.gov.in/iae.php) is an annual astronomical reference; [Indian Institute of Astrophysics](https://www.iiap.res.in/) and [IUCAA](https://www.iucaa.in/en/) document research facilities and outreach; UNESCO's [Jantar Mantar, Jaipur listing](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1338/) documents one historic observatory. None inventories all observatories, skywatching sites or historic instruments. | Separate historical instruments, operational facilities, outreach venues and calculated sky events. Cite ephemeris year, location or reference frame and time convention for claims that depend on them; don't copy annual tables without checking terms. Check facility access and outreach schedules directly; no entry or visibility guarantees. |
| Astrology and calendar culture | PAC [*Rashtriya Panchang*](https://packolkata.imd.gov.in/rp.php) is a multilingual national almanac; IGNCA [manuscript catalogs](https://ignca.gov.in/divisionss/kalanidhi/reference-library/non-print-material/catalogue-of-manuscripts/) and the Sangeet Natak Akademi inventory provide leads for texts, festivals and practices. Add dated regional almanacs, relevant manuscript repositories and consenting local practitioners. PAC standardization does not represent every regional calendar, observance or jyotiṣa tradition. | Document astrology as a historical or living belief/practice, not an astronomical finding or predictive service. Distinguish calculated calendar dates from locally observed festival dates. Record tradition, edition, year/era, locality, time zone and applicable conventions; corroborate annual dates with local organizers. Link to manuscript shelf marks/folios and almanac editions, and clear images, translations, tables and recordings separately. |

### Shared geography and rights

- Use the [Local Government Directory](https://lgdirectory.gov.in/) for current state, UT and district identifiers. Retain the historical district name and boundary vintage alongside any modern match; don't force a cross-border practice or a river into a single jurisdiction. Check [Survey of India products](https://onlinemaps.surveyofindia.gov.in/Home.aspx) and [geospatial guidelines](https://onlinemaps.surveyofindia.gov.in/GeospatialGuidelines.aspx) before publishing maps. Naming and administrative codes are not boundary geometry or routing points.
- Check [Open Government Data Platform India](https://data.gov.in/) for cataloged releases and the applicable [GODL–India notification](https://data.gov.in/sites/default/files/Gazette_Notification_OGDL.pdf). Apply GODL only where the resource actually carries that grant. Capture the publisher, dataset/asset version, license or permission URL and required attribution. A portal's public visibility, downloadable PDF or map service is not itself permission to scrape, mirror or publish derivatives. Never bypass accounts, access controls or limits.
- Default unclear or restricted material to **citation/link only**: write original, sourced summaries where appropriate rather than copying prose, scans, tables or images. For reusable material record any commercial-use, adaptation and share-alike conditions; check compatibility with the intended distribution. Record licenses separately for datasets, text, geodata, media and code. For photos retain creator, original file link, license, edits and derivative terms in `src/place-photos.json`; don't label a context photo as the site. Keep [OpenStreetMap's ODbL attribution](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) separate from editorial source credits.
- Legal permission and ethical consent are separate gates. Limit public precision for vulnerable places, species and communities. Remove revealing coordinates from citations as needed (withhold the link if it exposes the location), image metadata, static files, downloads and routing inputs, not merely the visible map. Don't put unpublished sensitive records or consent paperwork in the public repo; record only an approved reference and release decision there.

## Ingestion and review

1. **Register a source before importing.** Assign a source key and maintainer; record custodian, title, release/vintage, source ID/URL, retrieved date, format, geographic/temporal scope, expected blind spots, rights evidence, sensitivity and refresh cadence. Mark `reusable`, `link-only` or `hold`. Prefer approved versioned CSV/GeoJSON/API releases; for PDFs, archives and oral sources use a small, manually checked extraction with volume/page, shelf mark, accession or consent reference. Keep checksummed inputs in staging only where rights allow storage. Log broken links or login requirements; do not treat them as evidence of absence.
2. **Normalize into a draft, not directly into the app.** Keep an internal stable ID, source-native IDs, record type (place, area, object, occurrence, practice, event or reference), theme tags, names in original scripts and aliases, jurisdiction codes/vintage, and claim-level citations with date ranges and confidence notes. Keep public location and location precision (`verified public entrance`, `site`, `approximate area`, `withheld`) separate from evidence geometry. Never invent coordinates for a dish, community, manuscript or region. Flag possible duplicates by ID, aliases, district and proximity; reviewers resolve them, preserving source identities and alternate names.
3. **Review evidence, rights and sensitivity.** A domain or regional reviewer checks factual claims, chronology, names, geometry and inconsistent evidence against page-level sources; seek an independent source for contested claims and record disagreements rather than averaging dates or positions. A rights reviewer checks license and attribution; consult custodians or community reviewers for living traditions and restricted sites. Use `draft → in review → approved discovery` or `hold`; record reason, reviewer and checked date. If rights, consent or safe publicity cannot be established, don't publish the affected fields or record.
4. **Publish small batches with a manifest.** Export only approved public fields to the editorial atlas (`src/atlas.js`; guide notes `src/field-notes.js`). Check citation coverage, unique IDs, legal credits, coordinates/precision, links, and that withheld fields cannot leak through static HTML, bundle, photos or maps. Review added/changed/withdrawn totals and inspect sample rendered guides before deployment. Record release version and source versions so changes and corrections can be traced. Keep an issue path for bad facts, outdated information, privacy and takedown requests; unpublish sensitive claims promptly pending review.
5. **Promote routes only after a separate access review.** A public discovery entry has no route eligibility by default. For `src/catalog.js` and route data, verify the legal visitor entrance, current hours/closures, seasonal restrictions and permits, parking or pickup, walking approach, activity duration and realistic travel estimate. Date the check and recheck before promotion or after a change; send unconfirmed access back to discovery-only. Do not route to an approximate marker, research locality or culturally restricted venue. Run the unit tests, static build and browser checks before releasing planner changes.

## Milestones and coverage reporting

| Milestone | Exit criteria |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Audit the baseline | Freeze an LGD snapshot and create a 36-jurisdiction × 8-theme coverage matrix. Audit existing atlas entries for source, rights and precision; list map-only, link-only and unresolved material. Assign a maintainer and a review path for each theme. |
| 2. Pilot the workflow | Start with Adilabad/Telangana and Bengaluru/Karnataka, plus an underrepresented Northeast or island jurisdiction to test transferability. Review sample records across all eight themes across the pilots (not necessarily eight per pilot), including an official list, a report or archive scan, and a local-language source. Exercise deduplication, a link-only record, a location withheld and a discovery record denied route eligibility. |
| 3. Nationwide first pass | For every jurisdiction and theme, log the national and/or local sources checked, scope and dates reviewed, gaps and next step. Publish only reviewed discoveries; empty cells may remain. Prioritize vernacular and offline collections, state/UT custodians and underrepresented districts before adding more records from already well-documented centers. Call this a **nationwide first pass**, not a comprehensive inventory. |
| 4. Deepen and maintain | Expand through district batches and partners, with an explicit district denominator and boundary vintage. Review fresh source editions and matrix gaps quarterly, dated calendar events each edition, and visit access before planner releases or on a closure report. Retire stale or retracted records, document corrections and report changes alongside unresolved rights and sensitivity holds. |

Report **jurisdictions and districts reviewed / in scope**, approved public record counts by theme and jurisdiction, sources reviewed (including edition/year), and last review dates. Distinguish `not researched`, `reviewed—no publishable source located`, `hold`, and `approved coverage`; count route-verified stops separately. No empty cell or zero count implies the place, species, food or practice does not exist.
