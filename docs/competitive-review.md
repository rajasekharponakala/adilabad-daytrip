# Fieldday India: competitive review

**Follow-up:** See the [hands-on traveller benchmark](traveller-benchmark.md) for attempts to complete the same free-day task through public products. Fieldday’s integrated release was also exercised on the live Pages site: searching **Prakrit inscription** led through the Jainath guide to a plan returning to Adilabad railway station at **11:52 IST**; **Peninsular Gneiss** led through Lalbagh’s guide to a plan returning to Cubbon Park metro at **14:48 IST**. These are planner estimates, not timed field visits.

**Desk review · 22 November 2026 · repository baseline `bc63e19`.** Scope: India-first discovery and one-day planning, with global benchmarks for interpretation, routing and field observation. The links below are publisher pages, public product surfaces or operator documentation. “Observed” means visible there or in the baseline source; a documented feature is not an independently tested feature. This is not a survey of every paid tier, an on-site access audit or evidence of market demand. Where a capability was not found on the sampled pages, that does not establish its absence from the whole product.

**Recommendation (inferred):** Keep national discovery and local itinerary coverage separate. Make the two existing hubs better at answering *“What fits my free day, what should I notice, and what must I verify before leaving?”* Competing on more pins, richer maps or a bigger gallery is a weaker bet than making the handoff from a good field guide to a realistic visit dependable.

## Observed — Fieldday baseline

- **Discovery:** 48 editorial records covering all 28 states and eight union territories; browse by text, state/UT and theme, and save ideas in browser storage. All 48 have static, no-JavaScript field guides with attributed photos, observation prompts, access checks and sources. Atlas cards distinguish “In the planner,” “Needs confirmation” and “Discovery only.” An eligible atlas card opens its hub, not an itinerary containing that stop. ([README](../README.md), [atlas data](../src/atlas.js), [guide text](../src/field-notes.js), [UI](../src/app.js))
- **Planning:** Adilabad and Bengaluru only, with six eligible stops total (one appointment-dependent) and preset starts. Date, return window, car/foot, pace, indoor/outdoor, ranked interests and optional lunch drive buffered round trips. Results include a stop timeline, skip-and-replan, PDF/print and a schematic; routing uses bundled OSM/OSRM estimates, not live routing. Lunch is time reserved, not a venue recommendation. The app has no account or analytics; its inputs stay on the device unless a user chooses to take information elsewhere. Static HTML and bundled routes are not a promise of offline browser access. ([Catalog](../src/catalog.js), [planner](../src/planner.js), [UI](../src/app.js), [README](../README.md))
- **Access:** Gandhi Park and Jainath Temple use provisional windows. Kala Ashram is excluded until the visitor enters a host-confirmed interval for the selected date. Kuntala and Pochera are discovery-only; Pochera’s road endpoint snapped about 810 m from the falls pin in the route audit. Bengaluru’s Cubbon Park logic excludes published recurring closure days. Result text discloses a shared source-review date and no field audit; none of this is live access or a step-free guarantee. ([Catalog](../src/catalog.js), [planner](../src/planner.js), [README](../README.md))
- **Interpretation:** Seven locally shipped, on-demand schematic Geo Lab models illustrate processes, not measured terrain, forecasts or reconstructions. Guides distinguish UNESCO Tentative List citations from inscription. ([README](../README.md), [atlas data](../src/atlas.js), [Geo Lab](../src/geo-lab.js))

**Observed spot check — Kuntala:** The national attraction page describes Adilabad in its text but is filed under a Karimnagar URL and publishes a map latitude of `18.440826…`; Fieldday’s falls reference is at `19.28548`. The district page places the falls about 12 km from Neredikonda and gives a 45 m drop (roughly the national page’s 147 ft). Neither the agreement on height nor the competing pins verifies a public entrance. **Inference:** Hold routing until the access point and approach are checked; do not resolve the discrepancy by picking a more official-looking pin.

## Ranked opportunities — inferred, not validated demand

Priority reflects impact on the one-free-day job, consequences of bad data, and effort the current static site can sustain. Start with the existing hubs, not a third one.

| Rank | Next step | Scope and acceptance check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1 · Establish visit confidence** | Give each eligible stop a separately identified public entrance or meeting point, approach facts (steps, surface and walking distance where checked), a source for access, and a last-checked date. Keep provisional, confirmed-for-date and discovery-only states distinct. | Moderate editorial/field effort; little new infrastructure. Audit the six eligible stops first. Keep waterfalls discovery-only until the responsible authority and a field check establish a permitted approach and usable route. Test that an absent Kala Ashram confirmation never yields a stop. |
| **2 · Make the planner explain its choices** | Link every timed stop to its guide, show a plain-language reason it fits, and show specific blockers (appointment, scheduled closure, or insufficient time) where known. Carry interest into the hub from discovery without promising that any saved stop will fit. | Small-to-moderate UI work; reuse current rules and skip/replan. Check an indoor-only day without Kala confirmation, a Cubbon closure date and a short walking window. Users should be able to tell why a stop is absent and what change can help without guessing. |
| **3 · Improve the existing take-along plan** | Build on print/PDF: include the usable meeting point, visit and return times, verification notes and an observation prompt. Offer an explicit external map handoff only for validated endpoints; do not include private visit details in the link. | Small UI/template work. Save as PDF, reopen in airplane mode, and confirm that the core itinerary is useful without a data connection. External links may still need connectivity; that is not offline navigation. |
| **4 · Connect interpretation to the field** | Link existing guides and Geo Lab processes in both directions where relevant; put a short text/diagram explanation beside 3D, and pilot vetted eBird/iNaturalist links only where they add value. Test Telugu visit labels or a short Telugu checklist with Adilabad readers before promising locale-wide translation. | Moderate editorial effort. Start with a few guide/model pairs and a few public nature sites. Each should still communicate what to look for from a permitted spot without 3D, a sign-in or a third-party request. |

Before adding another routing hub, run the same one-day task with a small group in both existing hubs and compare proposed routes to local checks. Track, in consented sessions rather than analytics, whether participants identify an unconfirmed stop, find the correct meeting point, and can read the return plan under poor connectivity. Require reviewed endpoints and access windows per proposed stop, an owner for rechecks, and representative timed trips in the offered modes before expanding.

## Realistic exclusions

- **No pan-India routing promise, arbitrary origins, transit/cycling, multi-day logistics, bookings or turn-by-turn navigation in this phase.** Each new mode and hub adds route and access maintenance. Link out rather than rebuild commodity navigation.
- **No live weather, closure or fee guarantees; no blanket accessibility rating.** Link to operator notices and publish dated, specific access observations or “unknown.” A guide, official listing, route snap or map marker does not establish that the entrance is open, lawful or accessible.
- **No accounts, public reviews, collaboration backend, live sighting ingestion or species-ID uploads.** They would change the local-first privacy model and require moderation, licensing review and location safeguards. Never republish sensitive wildlife coordinates or map nonpublic cultural sites without permission.
- **No exhaustive geoheritage register, automated protected-status badges, photorealistic nationwide 3D or bulk copying of competitor content.** Link to primary records, keep distinct designation types explicit, credit media under its actual license, and expand lightweight interpretation only where it improves the visit.
